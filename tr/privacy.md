# Privacy Policy for TrainTracker

**Effective date:** 09/10/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **TrainTracker** handles your information. We built this app to be completely anonymous. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
We do not collect, request, or store any personal data. This means:
- We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
- We do not use accounts, logins, or passwords.
- We do not track your device or your personal identity.

## 2. Information Handled While Sharing
The only information the app handles is the **live position of the train** you choose to share.
- **Anonymous ID:** When you start sharing, Firebase creates a temporary, anonymous ID. This ID cannot be linked to you or your device.
- **Local Storage:** Any settings or preferences are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers.
- **Temporary Server Storage:** While you are actively sharing, your train's position is sent to our database so other users can see it. **This data is deleted immediately** when you stop sharing or when your train reaches the end of its route. Nothing is kept permanently.

## 3. No One Can See Who You Are
Other users can only see the train's position. They cannot see your name, your device, or your location. You are completely anonymous.

## 4. Data Deletion
You are in full control of your data. 
- When you press "Stop sharing," your train's position is instantly removed from the server.
- When your train reaches the end of the line, the sharing ends automatically and the data is removed.
- If your connection drops, the data is removed.
- You can also clear all local app data at any time by going to your device's settings and clearing the app's cache.

## 5. Third-Party Services
We use Google Firebase for the real-time database and anonymous authentication. Firebase processes the temporary train positions while sharing is active. You can read Google's privacy policy here: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 6. Children's Privacy
Because we do not collect any personal information, this app is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or store any data from anyone, including children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Since we don't collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly, so we encourage you to review this page occasionally.
